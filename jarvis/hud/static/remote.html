One-time setup for voice

iOS only lets a web app use the microphone over a secure connection. Your Mac makes its own certificate for that. Install it once on this device:

  1. Download the JARVIS profile Tap Allow, then Close.
  2. Open Settings, tap Profile Downloaded near the top, then Install.
  3. In Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings, switch on JARVIS.
  4. Come back here and tap Continue.
