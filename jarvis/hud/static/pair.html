Companion

Pair your iPhone or iPad.

Ask JARVIS anything, answer his questions, run protocols and see what he's saying, from any iPhone or iPad on your home Wi-Fi.

The companion is off. Turning it on lets devices on your home network pair with this Mac. Nothing is reachable from the internet, and each device needs its own pairing.

  1. Open the Camera on your iPhone or iPad and point it at the code.
  2. Tap the JARVIS link that appears. It opens in Safari and pairs.
  3. Add to Home Screen (Share ▸ Add to Home Screen) for a full-screen app.

Same Wi-Fi as this Mac. Typing works, and so does the 🎤 dictation key on the keyboard. If the link won't open, type in Safari instead.
