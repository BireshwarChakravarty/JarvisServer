Companion

Pair your iPhone or iPad.

Talk to JARVIS, hear him answer, run protocols and see what he's saying, from your iPhone or iPad: at home, or anywhere with Tailscale.

The companion is off. Turning it on lets devices on your home network pair with this Mac. Nothing is reachable from the internet, and each device needs its own pairing.

  1. Open the Camera on your iPhone or iPad and point it at the code.
  2. Tap the JARVIS link that appears. It opens in Safari and pairs.
  3. To talk to him, tap the microphone in the companion and follow the one-time setup (a small certificate from this Mac, so iOS allows the microphone).
  4. Add to Home Screen (Share ▸ Add to Home Screen) for a full-screen app.

Same Wi-Fi as this Mac. If the link won't open, type in Safari instead.

Works anywhere your iPhone has internet, through your private Tailscale network: keep Tailscale on, on the iPhone. The Mac must stay on and awake.

Use it away from home too (free)
  1. Install Tailscale on this Mac and from the App Store on your iPhone.
  2. Sign in to both with the same account.
  3. Come back here and choose Anywhere, then scan the code.
It's a private, encrypted network between your own devices; nothing is opened to the internet.
